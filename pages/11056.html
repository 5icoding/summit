    In this task, students design an experiment using Fast Plants to test how well they remove copper and zinc toxins from the environment.

    There are 3 parts: 1) preparation and participation in a Socratic Seminar on electronic waste (e waste), 2) designing and conducting a multi-week experiment using Fast Plants and copper and zinc toxins, and 3) analyzing and communicating the results from the experiment in a lab report.
