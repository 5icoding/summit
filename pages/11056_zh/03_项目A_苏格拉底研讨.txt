Questions for Socratic Seminar



Opening Questions: Would you ever do something dangerous to make money?  



Why is e-waste a problem?

What is e-waste? Why is e-waste dangerous?

How does this affect developing countries like Ghana, India, & China?

Is this just?

How is e-waste a cycle?



Who should be blamed for e-waste?  

Why do you buy new electronics?
Who has the greatest responsibility in producing e-waste? (manufacturers, consumers, workers, governments)

Who has the greatest responsibility in disposing end e-waste? (manufacturers, consumers, workers, governments)

Should the government have the right to restrict manufacturers from producing toxic electronics?

Should the government have the right to restrict consumers from buying and disposing toxic electronics?
What should we do about the e-waste problem?

Would you be willing to pay more for less toxic electronics?

If technology is constantly improving, is it possible that we will ever end e-waste?

Is preventing e-waste our choice or our duty?
