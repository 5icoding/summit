任务上下文：
Excited for the new iPhone 6? Have you heard the rumors about the iWatch? Don’t you wish you had a pair of Google glasses? Already tired of your Galaxy S5? Before you run off to the store to buy the latest and greatest smart phone, you should pause and take a moment to think about where your new phone is coming from and what you are going to do with your old phone. 140 million cell phones are thrown out worldwide each year and those phones contain hundreds of chemicals that are toxic to humans and damage our environment.
在这项任务中，您将作为硅谷环境保护局（ EPA ）的研究人员团队开展工作。苹果和谷歌的高管来找你进行研究，并计划清理戴利城、红木城和圣何塞垃圾填埋场的数百万部旧手机、电脑和电视机中的毒素。为了制定清理计划，您首先需要设计并使用Fast Plants进行实验，以去除垃圾填埋场中的电子废物毒素。
